Runs every model over the file above, or over whatever the microphone last recorded, and reports what each one costs. Recording yourself for ten seconds says far more about which to use than a benchmark clip does. The audio is pushed all at once rather than at speaking pace, so first text is how long a model needs before it will commit to anything, not the delay you see live; speed is decode time over audio length, and anything under 1.00x keeps up with a conversation.
